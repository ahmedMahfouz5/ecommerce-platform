E-Commerce Platform deployed!

Access the services via port-forward (no Ingress configured):

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "ecommerce.backend.fullname" . }} 8000:8000
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "ecommerce.frontend.fullname" . }} 8080:8080

Then:

  curl http://localhost:8000/api/v1/health/live
  curl http://localhost:8000/api/v1/health/ready
  curl http://localhost:8000/api/v1/products

  # Or through the frontend nginx proxy:
  curl http://localhost:8080/api/v1/products
